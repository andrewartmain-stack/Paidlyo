Paidlyo · Design Tokens v1.1

Warm, grounded,
and quietly confident.

A warm-neutral system for time tracking and invoicing. No blue anywhere. One deep evergreen accent carries every primary action. The canvas is parchment-warm, the ink is charcoal, and the type is clean geometric sans. It stays light on the eyes and credible for financial workflows.

01 · Colors

The palette is 85–90% warm neutrals. The accent (#1f4d3a) is the only saturated colour, and it should appear on less than 5% of pixels — primary actions, links, focus rings, and active navigation. Never use it for body text or large decorative fills. No blue is used at any point in the system.

02 · Typography

Two clean sans-serifs. Manrope handles headings — geometric, modern, and quietly confident without being decorative. Inter handles all UI and body copy — designed for screens, it keeps dense tables and financial data legible at 12–14px. This pairing reads as “modern business” through restraint, not novelty.

Families

--font-heading · Manrope 500–700
Turn hours into invoices.

Use for display, h1–h3, and large numbers. Weight 600. Tight negative tracking on sizes above 24px.

--font-body · Inter 400–600

Use for body, UI labels, buttons, tables, inputs, and captions. Weight 400 for body, 500 for buttons and labels, 600 for emphasis.

Control sizing. Buttons and inputs use a 36px height (padding: 0 16px; font-size: 14px). Compact table rows are 40–44px. Card padding is 24px. Section gaps are 64–96px. Focus rings are 2px Evergreen with a 2px offset — always visible, never removed.

04 · Principles

Warm neutral, always

The canvas is parchment-warm, not blue-white. Ink is charcoal, not pure black. This is what keeps the product light on the eyes during long invoicing sessions.

One accent, no blue

Evergreen is the only saturated colour. It is stable, financial, and distinct from the sea of blue SaaS products. Use it sparingly on primary actions and links only.

Type does the hierarchy

Weight and size carry the hierarchy — not colour, not boxes, not icons. If everything is bold, nothing is. Use 600 sparingly.

Quiet depth

No drop shadows. Elevation comes from 1px borders and background tint alone. This is what keeps the interface light and modern.

# Product

## Positioning
A simpler, cheaper Harvest for freelancers and small agencies: track time, turn unbilled time
into a branded invoice in one click, get paid online. Built to make switching from Harvest painless.

**Target user:** Harvest customers hit by the price increase (freelancers, 2–20 person agencies).

## Competitor summary
| Area | Harvest | Keito | Invoisin | Us (plan) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Time tracking | Timer, timesheet, calendar sync, approvals | Same + AI-agent time | Timer, manual entry | Timer + timesheet (MVP); approvals, calendar later |
| Expenses | Receipts, mileage | Expenses + receipts | Basic | V1: expenses + receipt upload |
| Invoicing | Auto from time, templates, payments | Approved time/expenses → lines, pay online | One-click from unbilled time, drafts, branded PDF | MVP: one-click from unbilled time, editable drafts, branded print/PDF, pay online |
| Rates | Person / project / task | Project / person | Client / project / task | MVP: project & task rates; person rates with teams (V1) |
| Reporting | Profitability, budget alerts, utilization | Billable vs internal, utilization, unbilled | By client/project/task/teammate, CSV | MVP: unbilled, outstanding, hours + CSV; budgets V1 |
| Client portal | — | — | No-login invoice view/download | MVP: no-login invoice link |
| Tax & currency | Via integrations | — | VAT/GST, multi-currency, credit notes | MVP: per-invoice tax + currency per client; credit notes V1 |
| Integrations | 40+ | AI assistant, Asana, calendar, Stripe ACH | Calendar, QuickBooks/Xero beta, CSV | Stripe + CSV first; others later |
| Migration | — | Harvest importer + compatible API | Harvest importer with preview | MVP: Harvest importer with preview |
| Pricing | Per seat + usage fees | $19 solo / $49 team flat | $9/seat (annual), unlimited | **Open decision** |

## What we must match (table stakes for switchers)
1. Timer + weekly timesheet with client/project/task and billable flag
2. Project/task hourly rates and billable vs non-billable time
3. Invoice from unbilled time in one click, editable before sending
4. Branded invoice, no-login client link, PDF download
5. Online payment (Stripe) + manual payment recording
6. Tax rate and per-client currency on invoices
7. Unbilled / outstanding reports with CSV export
8. **Harvest importer** (clients, projects, tasks, people, time entries, invoices) with preview

## Where we can win
- **Price and no usage fees.** Unlimited clients, projects, invoices.
- **Simplicity.** Fewer screens than Harvest, fast timesheet.
- **Painless switch.** Importer with a preview of new / updated / duplicate records.
- **Free no-login client portal** (Harvest does not highlight one).

## Explicitly not now
AI agent time tracking, timesheet approvals, calendar sync, QuickBooks/Xero, public API,
mileage, 40+ integrations. Revisit after paying customers ask.

## Pricing options (decide before SaaS billing feature)
- A: Flat like Keito, but cheaper (e.g. $12 solo / $29 team).
- B: Per seat, cheaper than Invoisin's monthly price, with a free solo tier (limited clients).
- C: Free solo forever + flat team plan (aggressive acquisition from Harvest's free-plan users).
